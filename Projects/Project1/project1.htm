Website Critiques

Questions:

  1. What was your first impression of this site?
  2. How does this site organize the flow of information? (Do they have links on the left and text on the right? Some other scheme? No scheme?)
  3. Does the site structure make sense or does it confuse you? Why?
  4. Does the site graphics/layout/multimedia enhance the site or detract from it? Why?
  5. How long did it take for the home page to load? Was the load time reasonable?
  6. Was the text color and size readable? If a background color or image was used, did it enhance or detract from the site?
  7. Did the site have a navigation bar or other navigation tool? Was the navigation well done?
  8. Did the site have a color scheme or look and feel that was repeated on all
    9. the pages?
  9. How does the site respond to smaller window sizes (responsive design)?
  10. What does this site offer that was unusual or very well done?

Trader Joe's Website Critiques

Trader Joe's

  1. My first impression of this site is that it is organized, simple, and festive.
  2. The site organizes flow of information with tabs for products on the left with a search bar on the top right side of the screen. They have included links to recipes and products with a shopping list feature on the top right side of the screen too.
  3. The site structure makes sense. It is well organized and easy to navigate.
  4. The layout of the site enhances the experience because it is easy to find everything. The graphics are cohesive with the branding which also enhances the experience.
  5. The load time was very fast. Within a second the page loaded.
  6. The text color and size was readable. I thought the background color enhanced the site and allowed for easier reading.
  7. The site had a navigation bar that made it easy to find products and recipes quickly.
  8. Yes. Although, the home page was custom to reflect the season. The pages within the navigation bar were cohesive with Trader Joe's brand. The color scheme, layout, fonts, and graphics were repeated on all pages.
  9. The site is very responsive to small windows. The page automatically adjusted to account for the smaller window. I could easily read and see everything clearly even within the small window.
  10. I was impressed with the layout most of all. The webpage is very well organized. I am able to find everything I need within the home page alone. Not only that but the pages don't feel crowded either. It's well spaced, clean, and easily navigatable.
